### 5. COMPLETE SUMMARY OF PDF DISCREPANCIES & TYPOS ACROSS ALL 3 PDFS

#### A. 100K PDF (`anchorbolt100ksingleleg.pdf`)
1. **Page 1 Top Table Pasted Multi-Leg Data**: Listed `Nb = 4`, `Dcl = 41'-0.5625"`, `39 x 39 in` pedestal, `e = 13.5 in`, and `Mu = 5673.437 kip-ft` from 100K Multi-Leg instead of Single-Leg (`Nb = 20`, `rb = 10.75 ft`).
2. **Missing `0.6` Wind Factor on `Mu` (`4847.67` vs. `2903.6953 kip-ft`)**: Page 2 used raw $M_{\text{wind}} \times 1.667 = 4847.67\text{ kip-ft}$ without the `0.6` ASD factor (which 250K PDF later corrected).
3. **Bolt Circle Lever Arm (`D = 21.0 ft` vs. `21.5 ft`)**: Page 2 used ringwall centerline diameter `21.0 ft` (`0.67 × 21 = 14.07 ft`), whereas Pages 3–13 specify actual bolt circle diameter `2 × 10.75 = 21.5 ft` (`0.67 × 21.5 = 14.405 ft`).
4. **Unfactored Wind Shear (`20.683 kips` vs. `53.4318 kips`)**: Page 9 used unfactored wind shear without the `30 psf × Cf` minimum wind pressure floor.

#### B. 250K PDF (`anchorbolt250ksingleleg.pdf`)
1. **Page 7 Arc & Chord Body Typos**: Page 7 body swapped/mismatched Arc Spacing and left 100K's `40.35 in` for Chord Spacing; **Page 12 Summary Table** corrected both to `26.18 in` and `26.15 in`, matching our software exactly.
2. **Page 8 & Page 12 Edge Distance Typo (`e = 54 in`)**: Page 1 specifies `e = 3.0 in` ($\rightarrow e_{\text{clear}} = 2.1875\text{ in}$), while Page 8 & Page 12 accidentally typed `e = 54 in` ($\rightarrow 53.19\text{ in}$).
3. **Page 9 Unfactored Shear Substitution**: Page 1 lists factored `Vu = 86.4 kips` (matching our live `82.0907 kips`), but Page 9 plugged in unfactored `Vu = 44.5 kips` ($\rightarrow V_{\text{bolt}} = 1.24\text{ kips}$).

#### C. 500K PDF (`anchorbolt500ksingleleg.pdf`)
1. **Page 3 Moment Arithmetic Mismatch**: Wrote `0.6(6117.08) = Mu = 4976.0383 ft-kip`, whereas `0.6 × 6117.08 = 3670.25` (and `× 1.667 = 6118.30`).
2. **Pages 3–4 Uplift Lever Arm & Division Error**: Page 1's diagram shows the standard `0.67 D` lever arm (`20.435 ft`), but Page 3 wrote `T_total = Mu / rb` and Page 4 wrote `4976.0383 / 15.25 = 311.01 kips` (even `4976.0383 / 15.25` is `326.30`, while `4976.0383 / 16.0 = 311.00`). Our software consistently applies the standard `0.67 D` lever arm (`20.435 ft`).
3. **Pages 10–11 Copy-Paste from 100K PDF**: Page 1 specifies `Hef = 35 in` and Pages 4 & 9 computed 500K bolt forces, but Pages 10–11 left 100K's numbers pasted in (`T_bolt = 34.45 kips`, `V_bolt = 1.034 kips`, `Utilization = 0.470`, `hef = 96 in`, `phi*Nb = 1070 kips`, `Breakout Utilization = 0.032`). With Page 1's `hef = 35 in`, our software computes the true 500K breakout capacity (`235.72 kips`) and utilization (`0.1078`).
