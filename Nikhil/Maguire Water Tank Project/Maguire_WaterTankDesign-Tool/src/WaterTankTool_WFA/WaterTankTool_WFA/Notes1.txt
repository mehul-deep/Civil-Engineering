### 🚀 LATEST SESSION ACHIEVEMENTS: DUAL EXCEL OUTPUT ARCHITECTURE, CAD DIAGRAM REFINEMENT & COLUMN AUDIT

We achieved a major architectural expansion of the reporting and export subsystem, creating a Dual-Output Architecture that independently caters to fabrication shop floors and engineering plan reviewers, while refining CAD diagram precision and preparing a comprehensive column audit.

1. **Dual Excel Output Architecture (`Fabrication Output` vs. `Engineering Summary`)**
   • **Fabrication Output (`Output.xlsx`):** Renamed the top menu item from `"Export Output"` to **`"Fabrication Output"`** (`WaterTank.resx` and `WaterTank.Designer.cs`). This export preserves the strict **107-column manufacturing schedule** untouched, ensuring 100% backward compatibility with company CNC machines, ERP macros, and shop floor cutting/drilling equipment. Populates Standpipe Base Cone (`B1-B5`) and Cylinder (`C1-C18`) dimensions, Base Plate specs (`BPDIA`, `BPOR`, `BPIR`, `BPDEG`, `BPTHK`, `BPQ`), and Anchor Bolt drilling coordinates (`ABQ`, `ABHQ`, `ABHSD`).
   • **Engineering Summary (`EngineeringSummary.xlsx`):** Created a brand new **`"Engineering Summary"`** menu item and calculation export engine (`ExportEngineeringSummary.cs`). Generates a standalone, executive-ready calculation report with soft blue header banners (`#D9E1F2`), bold grey labels, and clean cell borders across 5 audit sections:
     1) *Project & Tank Geometry Overview:* Designation, Capacity, Base Cone & Column dimensions.
     2) *Governing Structural Loads & Demands:* Overturning Moment (`Mu`), Axial Load (`Pu`), Base Shear (`Vu`), and Ultimate Load Factor (`1.667`).
     3) *Anchor Bolt Breakout Check (ACI 318-19):* Bolt counts, embedment depth (`hef`), strengths (`f'c`, `Fy`, `Fu`), demands per bolt (`Nua`, `Vua`), and an automated **Combined Interaction Ratio** check (outputting `OK` vs `EXCEEDS CAPACITY`).
     4) *Base Plate Bearing Check (AISC LRFD):* Radii, supporting area (`A2`), bearing stress (`fp`), required vs. actual thickness (`treq`), and an automated **Plate Compactness Status** alert (outputting `COMPACT / OK` vs alerting `CHECK THICKNESS`).
     5) *Material & Geotechnical Properties:* Steel grade name (`Testing 1`), modulus of elasticity (`E`), densities, and tensile yield/ultimate stresses.

2. **Base Plate CAD Diagram Refinement (`BasePlateParameters.cs`)**
   • Fixed a visual clipping issue on the left preview diagram (`_picBoltDiagram`) where the `Dcone` (Diameter) label was previously drawn at `yDim + 3f` (3 pixels below the horizontal dimension line), causing it to get cut off at the bottom edge.
   • Updated Y-coordinate positioning to `yDim - textSize.Height - 2f`, placing the label cleanly **above** the horizontal dimension line so it sits centered right on top of the dimension line and is 100% visible!
