2. Math Engine (FootingEquations.cs)
Flawlessly implemented the 8-step logic from `Footingmultileg.pdf`:
- Step 1: Calculated Compression Side Load ($P_c = 520.2$ kip) including dynamic overturning tension and Pedestal self-weight.
- Step 2 & 3: Computed Required Footing Area and verified applied bearing pressure ($2.85$ ksf).
- Step 6: Computed Sliding Check Factor of Safety ($FS = \mu P / V_{leg} > 1.5$).
- Step 7: Executed Two-Way Punching Shear Check:
  - Calculated effective depth $d$ and critical perimeter $b_o$.
  - Found upward soil reaction inside the perimeter and calculated net factored punching shear force ($V_{u,punch} = 435.1$ kip).
  - Computed ultimate shear stress ($v_u$) and compared it against ACI capacity ($\phi v_c = \phi \times 4\sqrt{f'_c}$).
- Step 8: Calculated total allowable Punching Shear Force Capacity and performed final Demand/Capacity verification.
